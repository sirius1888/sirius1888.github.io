# Store icons

- `appstore.svg`, `googleplay.svg`, `appgallery.svg`: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons), CC0-1.0; license in `SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`. App Store and AppGallery use their brand colours from the collection metadata. Google Play uses the monochrome version with theme-aware contrast.
- `rustore.svg`: the original square logo from the [official RuStore website](https://www.rustore.ru/_next/static/media/Logo.60d45862.svg), used to identify the store.

Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners.
